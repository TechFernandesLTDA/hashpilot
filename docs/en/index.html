Before you start

The default wallet belongs to the author: leaving it in place sends payouts to the author. Enter your own public address to receive payouts yourself. Never enter a seed phrase or private key. With another wallet, the optional contribution of up to 5% of connected time can be disabled.

Only one RTX 3070 has been physically validated; other cards and six-GPU rigs still need real-world testing. Projections do not guarantee profit. An official hash does not establish miner safety; see the Defender guidance.
