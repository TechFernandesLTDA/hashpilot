## English

> We are looking for five people with Windows 10/11 x64 and an NVIDIA GPU to test the [HashPilot prerelease](https://github.com/TechFernandesLTDA/hashpilot). It tracks Pearl mining, telemetry and history. Physical validation so far covers one RTX 3070; the six-GPU scenario is simulated. >
> The prefilled wallet belongs to the author. Enter your own public wallet to receive payouts. The optional contribution of up to 5% of connected time can be disabled. >
> Share your environment, steps and observed outcome in the [test report](https://github.com/TechFernandesLTDA/hashpilot/issues/new?template=external_test.yml). No promised results or private screenshots are needed. Thank you!
